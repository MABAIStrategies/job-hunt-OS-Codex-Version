# Job Hunt OS Release Announcement

## Core Announcement

MAB AI Strategies is building Job Hunt OS: a platform-agnostic operating system for turning a job opportunity into a complete, truthful, role-specific application package and follow-through workflow.

Give it a job URL, screenshot, pasted job description, or target title. It produces current compensation context, tailored resume and cover letter assets, application route detection, follow-up drafts, interview prep, mock hiring-manager review, knowledge-gap planning, and a dashboard-ready tracking record.

Built first as a Codex-native skill repo. Designed for staffing teams, RPO and outplacement firms, and career-services teams that need scalable application support without sacrificing accuracy or credibility.
