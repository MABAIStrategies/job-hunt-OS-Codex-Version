# Follow-Up Engine

Start only after the user confirms that an application was submitted or asks to simulate post-submission follow-up.

## Default Sequence

- T0: recruiter outreach on the submission day
- T+1 day: hiring-manager outreach if no recruiter response
- T+3 days: peer discovery and outreach if neither recruiter nor hiring manager responds

## Message Objective

Ask for 15 minutes to get preparatory context. If the recipient is not open to a call, ask:

- What is the main thing you hope the person in this role will focus on or build?
- What is the biggest bottleneck facing the role or team right now?

## Rules

- Keep messages warm, specific, low-friction, and prepared.
- Avoid desperation and over-selling.
- Log drafts and sends separately.
- Do not send messages without explicit user approval.
