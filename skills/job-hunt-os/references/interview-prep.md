# Interview Prep Engine

Return:

- company brief
- role brief
- current company themes
- likely objections
- tailored value proposition
- 10 to 15 likely interview questions
- answer angles rooted in the user's record
- story bank mapped to competencies
- recruiter questions
- hiring-manager questions
- peer questions
- optional 30, 60, and 90 day framing

## Rules

- Tie answers to verified accomplishments.
- Do not invent stories.
- When the profile lacks proof, mark it as a preparation gap and route it to the knowledge-gap engine.
