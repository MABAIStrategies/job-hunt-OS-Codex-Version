# Freshness and Research

Treat mutable facts as stale until checked on the day of generation.

## Verify When Relevant

- job posting availability
- current application URL
- compensation signals
- remote, hybrid, or onsite status
- company priorities, product launches, funding, AI initiatives, and hiring themes
- ATS or application path
- current contacts only when outreach is requested

## Research Outputs

Return concise notes for:

1. Company context
2. Current role status
3. Best available apply path
4. Compensation evidence
5. Company themes and language
6. Application method classification
7. Confidence and open gaps

## Source Preference

Prefer official company pages, official job postings, SEC or investor materials, reputable compensation sources, and recent primary company announcements. Use secondary sources only when primary sources are unavailable, and label the confidence.
