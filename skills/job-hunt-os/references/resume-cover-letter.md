# Resume and Cover Letter Engine

## Resume Objective

Create the strongest truthful resume for the target role while preserving source-of-truth constraints.

## Immutable Resume Fields

- employer names
- titles actually held
- exact date ranges
- chronology
- education history

## Allowed Resume Optimization

- rewrite headline and summary
- reorder bullets by relevance
- sharpen bullet wording
- group skills for role fit
- mirror job language when truthful
- surface AI, automation, operating, sales, or technical evidence more clearly
- create derived bullets only when directly supported by verified facts

## Forbidden Resume Behavior

- fabricated scope
- fabricated certifications or awards
- fictional tools
- invented revenue or team-size numbers
- title inflation
- date expansion

## Cover Letter Objective

Create a concise company-specific letter that sounds like the user and improves interview odds.

## Cover Letter Structure

1. Hook tied to company context or role need
2. Two or three verified achievements mapped to requirements
3. Understanding of what the company likely cares about now
4. Direct close with interest and next-step language

## Design Defaults

- PDF by default.
- Use company brand colors only when reliably identified and useful.
- Preserve readability over decorative design.
- Prefer an executive branded version plus a clean ATS-safe version when helpful.
