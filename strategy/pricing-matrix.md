# MAB AI Strategies Internal Pricing Matrix

Status: Internal draft for market validation. Do not publish without review.

## Pricing Logic

Use B2B SaaS plus setup. Setup covers onboarding, workflow configuration, profile import, templates, admin training, and pilot measurement. Monthly subscription covers platform access, usage, support, and continuous prompt/workflow improvements.

## Draft Tiers

| Tier | Setup Fee | Monthly Subscription | Included Users | Included Resume Upgrades | Included Applications | ICP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Pilot | $2,500 | $750 | 3 | 100 | 150 | Small staffing or career-services pilot |
| Team | $7,500 | $2,500 | 10 | 500 | 750 | Staffing team standardizing application support |
| Growth/RPO | $15,000 | $6,500 | 30 | 2,000 | 3,000 | RPO or outplacement team running multiple programs |
| Enterprise | $35,000+ | $15,000+ | Custom | Custom | Custom | Enterprise staffing platform or university system |

## Add-Ons

- Custom workflow implementation: $5,000 to $25,000.
- White-label template pack: $7,500 to $20,000.
- Advanced analytics dashboard: $5,000 setup plus $1,500 monthly.
- Dedicated success review: $1,000 monthly.
- Private deployment review: custom.

## Guardrails

- Do not discount setup below the cost of onboarding and implementation.
- Keep enterprise pricing custom when data volume, integrations, or security review are unknown.
- Treat direct job-seeker pricing as out of scope for v1 unless GTM changes.
